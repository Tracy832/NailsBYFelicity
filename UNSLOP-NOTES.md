# Unslop pass: Nails by Felicity

## Direction

- **Product:** Manicure, pedicure and nail art enquiries for Felicity’s studio in Murang’a.
- **Character:** Warm, assured, expressive.
- **World:** A personal nail studio: warm ivory, berry polish, real sets photographed by Felicity.
- **Moments:** Her finished nail designs lead the page; WhatsApp is the direct booking path.
- **Signature:** The simple “F” monogram in the studio wordmark.

## Audit and applied changes

- Repeated decorative stars and sparkle icons competed with the portfolio photos → removed the filler marks and kept a single studio mark.
- Wide-tracked uppercase micro-labels made the service and location details harder to read → moved labels to sentence case with lighter tracking.
- Italic emphasis repeated through headings and labels → kept the serif display type and berry emphasis, with upright letterforms.
- The hero headline could describe many beauty businesses → named manicures, pedicures and nail art, with Murang’a in the supporting line.
- Several small controls used tight corners and catch-all transitions → aligned button sizing and corner treatment, and named the color and transform transitions.

## Kept as they are

- The warm ivory and berry palette, serif display type and asymmetric hero layout.
- Felicity’s real portfolio photographs and the five-page structure.
- Enquiry-based prices, appointment availability and directions; the site does not invent these details.
