# Unslop pass: Nails by Felicity

## Direction

- **Product:** Manicure, pedicure, acrylic and press-on enquiries for Felicity’s studio in Murang’a.
- **Character:** Warm, assured, expressive.
- **World:** A personal nail studio: warm ivory, berry polish and the vivid blue cloth visible in Felicity’s photographs.
- **Moments:** A real red stiletto set opens the home page; WhatsApp is the direct appointment path.
- **Signature:** Cobalt blue for the primary booking action and selected gallery filter, drawn from the portfolio photos.

## Audit and applied changes

- **Spacing:** Uneven small gaps and tight text rows in the service list and footer → aligned key gaps, row padding and control heights to the 4px scale.
- **Color:** Ivory and berry were used everywhere, leaving the portfolio’s blue backdrops disconnected → added one cobalt accent for booking and the selected gallery filter, while keeping surfaces warm and quiet.
- **Icons:** Sparkle marks and a floral glyph repeated as decoration → removed them; kept Lucide icons only where they explain navigation, location or messaging.
- **Copy:** Broad beauty slogans appeared across Home, About and Gallery → replaced them with manicure, pedicure, acrylic and press-on language, plus captions tied to Felicity’s real sets.
- **Type:** Wide-tracked label styles and italic emphasis repeated across the pages → set labels in sentence case and kept the display serif upright, with berry for emphasis.
- **Layout:** The hero showed two competing photos plus a stamp → gave one real red stiletto set the large image area and captioned it. Kept the asymmetric text-and-image split.
- **Details:** The contact page used a striped pseudo-map, while small body copy was difficult to read → removed the map mimic, increased supporting text sizes and used clear button corners.
- **Visuals:** The hero image repeated in a small inset and an unrelated stamp → kept a single portfolio image as the proof point; the cobalt accent follows the blue fabric already present in her photos.
- **Motion:** Several transitions used catch-all or longer effects → named button/filter properties and kept interaction movement small; no looping motion remains.

## Kept as they are

- The warm ivory and berry palette, serif display type and asymmetric hero layout.
- Felicity’s real portfolio photographs and the five-page structure.
- Enquiry-based prices, appointment availability and directions; the site does not invent these details.
